# Analysis plan (public excerpt)

This file reproduces the hypotheses, the decision rules and the timeline of the analysis plan behind the manuscript "Unseen-Attack Detection in the Internet of Medical Things: A Leave-One-Attack-Out Evaluation of Sequence Models, Energy Scores and Feature-Space Profiles". It is an excerpt of the version-controlled plan document kept by the authors (version 1.4, locked 18 November 2026; SHA-256 of that document `9c476f63c8e996f585c689f53b8f2467638225a025ab3550375571bb85bd74f4`, computed with a placeholder in the lock-receipt slot). The full document is not part of this repository.

## 1. Specification posture
- The five held-out classes (ARP spoofing, Recon-VulScan, malformed MQTT traffic, TCP/IP DoS-UDP, MQTT-DDoS publish flooding) were fixed in a configuration file dated 3 October 2026, nine days before the first runs.
- The first 45 runs (seeds 42 to 44) were collected on 12 and 14 October 2026. Hypotheses H1 to H4 and their verdict rules were written on 14 October 2026, after those runs and before any aggregation statistic had been computed. This is a weaker posture than a-priori registration, and it is reported as such in the manuscript.
- Further dated steps are listed in section 3.

## 2. Hypotheses and decision rules (as in the manuscript, Sections III-E and III-F)

### Hypotheses
Five hypotheses operationalise the four research questions of the manuscript. Each hypothesis is evaluated at the per-pair level (one pair per (model, held-out class) triple) and at the overall level (aggregated across the five held-out classes for each model). Decision rules are a four-tier scheme, applied to the paired bootstrap 95% confidence interval of the relevant statistic.

H1 (safety view, operationalises RQ1): TRACE-MoE's combined caught-any detection rate on each held-out attack class is at least 0.95. Per-pair decision rule: Supported (strong) if mean at least 0.95 and 95% CI lower bound at least 0.90; Supported (preliminary) if mean at least 0.95 and CI lower bound < 0.90; Inconclusive if mean < 0.95 and the CI straddles 0.95; Not supported if CI upper bound < 0.95. Overall verdict, applied in this order: Not supported (overall) if any class is Not supported; Inconclusive (overall) if at least two classes are Inconclusive; Supported (strong, overall) if all five classes are Supported (strong); Supported (preliminary, overall) if all five classes are at least Supported (preliminary); Mixed in all other cases (for example, four classes Supported (strong) and one Inconclusive).

H2 (energy versus MSP, operationalises part of RQ2): For a given architecture, the energy-score area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) exceeds the MSP AUROC against known-class windows by a positive mean margin. Per-pair decision rule: paired bootstrap 95% CI of (energy_AUROC minus MSP_AUROC) with Supported if the CI excludes zero on the positive side, Not supported (baseline wins) if the CI excludes zero on the negative side, Not supported (narrow null) if the CI brackets zero and is no wider than a 0.10 CI-width margin; Supported (preliminary) if the CI brackets zero, is wider than the margin and the mean is positive; and Inconclusive if the CI brackets zero, is wider than the margin and the mean is not positive. Overall verdict per architecture, applied in this order: Supported (strong) if at least three of the five class pairs are Supported; Supported (preliminary) if at least three pairs are at least Supported (preliminary) and at least one is only preliminary (a tier that no pair reaches in the results reported here); Not supported if at least three pairs are Not supported; Inconclusive if at least three pairs are Inconclusive; Mixed otherwise.

H3 (architecture comparison, operationalises part of RQ2): TRACE-MoE's energy-score AUROC exceeds each of the two baseline sequence-model architectures' energy-score AUROC against known-class windows. Per-pair and overall decision rules are identical to H2, substituting architecture pair for scoring-function pair; the overall verdict is formed per baseline architecture, over the five held-out classes.

H4 (closed-set degradation, operationalises RQ3): The macro-F1 on the 18 non-held-out classes degrades by at most 0.10 (10 percentage points) under leave-one-attack-out training relative to the full 19-class baseline. Per-pair decision rule: Supported if the paired bootstrap 95% CI of the degradation has mean at most 0.10 and upper bound at most 0.10; Not supported if mean > 0.10 and lower bound > 0.10; Inconclusive if the CI straddles 0.10. Overall verdict, formed across all 15 (model, held-out class) pairs and applied in this order: Not supported (overall) if at least two pairs are Not supported; Inconclusive (overall) if at least three pairs are Inconclusive; Supported (overall) if all 15 pairs are Supported; Mixed otherwise.

H5 (protocol-cluster reference profile, operationalises RQ4): The feature-space Mahalanobis score D_min, computed against per-protocol-family profiles in the PCRP fit on the training split alone, achieves mean AUROC at least 0.80 against known-class windows. Per-pair decision rule: Supported (strong) if mean at least 0.80 and 95% CI lower bound at least 0.75; Supported (preliminary) if mean at least 0.80 and CI lower bound in [0.60, 0.75); Inconclusive if mean in [0.50, 0.80) or if the CI straddles 0.80; Not supported if CI upper bound < 0.65 or mean < 0.65, a condition checked first. Overall verdict: Supported (strong) if at least four of five pairs are Supported (strong); otherwise Supported (preliminary) if at least three are at least Supported (preliminary); otherwise Inconclusive if at least two are not Not supported; otherwise Not supported.

The two flavours of Not supported in H2 and H3 distinguish precisely measured practical equivalence (narrow null) from a precisely measured baseline advantage (baseline wins); in the common four-tier vocabulary, 'Supported (strong)' maps to Supported and both flavours map to Not supported.

### Statistical Protocol
All hypotheses are evaluated with paired bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (10,000 resamples) across the five seeds per (model, held-out class) pair, or per (model, held-out class, baseline) pair. With five paired observations the minimum two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank p-value is 2/2^5 = 0.0625, so no per-pair test can reach p < 0.05; we therefore report no p-values, and every verdict rests on the confidence interval and the rules of the manuscript. A wide interval that straddles a decision boundary is always reported as inconclusive, never as a null result. the tables reports per-seed values; the tables report means and intervals. No per-class multiple-comparison correction is applied; the overall rules require a majority of class pairs (at least three of five) before an architecture-level or model-level claim. At pair level, a bare "supported" in H2 and H3 is already the strongest tier, and H4 uses only supported, inconclusive and not supported. No reported H2 or H3 pair reaches the Supported (preliminary) tier. All decision rules, the five held-out classes and the five-seed design were fixed before the five-seed aggregation reported here.

## 4. Exploratory analyses
Every analysis in `supplementary_analyses/` is post hoc and exploratory and does not change any verdict. ESA1 (threshold calibration) and ESA2 (agreement between the energy and MSP rankings) had decision rules fixed on 14 October 2026; the other analyses were specified on 20 and 21 November 2026 and are reported as exploratory in the manuscript.
